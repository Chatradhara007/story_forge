The storm broke on Tuesday night.

The wind came first, then the rain, then the sea itself, climbing the rocks and hammering on the cottage door. Keeper Calloway's green eyes never left the lamp. She stayed at the top of the tower all night, feeding the flame and winding the clockwork every hour.

In Port Wick, Silas Rudd spent the whole night of the storm in the harbour office, sending telegrams up and down the coast and keeping a lamp in the window for any boat that might make it in. He did not leave the office until dawn.

Tobias stood at the foot of the tower stairs with a storm lantern. When a shutter tore loose, he climbed out onto the gallery and clapped both hands over the broken latch, holding it closed until Ines could lash it with rope.

Near midnight, a wave broke the kitchen window. Ines slipped on the glass as she ran down to check on Pip, and she cut her wrist badly. She wrapped it in a dish towel and went back up the tower.

By morning the sea was grey and tired, and the light had not gone out.
