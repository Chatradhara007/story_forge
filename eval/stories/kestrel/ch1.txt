Chapter One

Relay Station Kestrel hung over the night side of Tarsis like a rusted wheel. It had been built for forty people. It held four.

Commander Yusra Adeyemi was forty-five and had been on Kestrel for three rotations, longer than anyone else would have stood it. She was a tall woman with grey eyes and a burn scar on her left forearm from a coolant fire on her first posting, which she never bothered to cover. On Day 212 of the rotation she was in the hub with a cup of something that was not coffee, reading the supply manifests for the third time.

Her engineer, Bastian Kowal, was thirty-one and wore a great black beard that he claimed kept his face warm in the cold sections. He was her sister's son. Yusra had signed off on his posting herself and regretted it twice a week, mostly when he sang.

The station's medic was Dr Ana Reyes, fifty and unflappable, who had delivered babies in orbit and set bones in freefall and considered Kestrel a holiday.

The fourth was Haruki Mori, the relief pilot, who flew the station's only shuttle. The Tern was a stubby white shuttle with a cracked heat shield that Bastian kept promising to fix.

"Supply drop is in sixteen days," Yusra said. "Until then, we are careful."

"We're always careful," said Bastian.

"We have one spare oxygen regulator," said Yusra. "One. If the main one goes, that's it until the drop. So we are careful."

Ana raised her mug. "To being careful," she said.
