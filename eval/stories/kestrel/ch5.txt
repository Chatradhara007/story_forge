Chapter Five

Day 220. The main regulator failed at four in the morning with a sound like a sigh.

Bastian was in the plant room within a minute, clean-shaven and already in his suit. He pulled the dead regulator out of its housing, took the spare oxygen regulator down from the shelf where it had always been kept, and fitted it in eleven minutes flat.

"There," he said. "Good as new."

The air began to move again. Yusra let out a breath she had been holding for six days.

Ana was on the medical deck, her leg up, monitoring their blood oxygen on the station's screens. "Everyone's fine," she reported. "Bastian's heart rate is a disgrace, but everyone's fine."

At noon the comm crackled. "Kestrel, Kestrel, this is the Tern," said Haruki Mori's voice, faint through the static. "I'm coming round the antenna. Have you got the kettle on?"

Nobody answered him.

Yusra rolled up the sleeve of her overall to wash, and looked for a moment at the old burn on her right forearm, shiny and pale under the hub lights.

Eight days to the drop, she thought. Only eight days.
