Chapter Six

The supply ship came on Day 228, exactly on time, which nobody on Kestrel had believed it would.

It brought food, spare suits, a new pilot, a new shuttle and a new oxygen regulator, still in its packing foam. Bastian installed the new regulator that afternoon beside the one he had fitted on Day 220, and stood back to admire them both.

"Two," he said. "We have two. I'm never letting either of them out of my sight."

Ana walked down to the docking bay to meet the ship with a cane, slowly but on her own feet. The splint had come off two days earlier, and she was already complaining about the cane.

The new shuttle was bright orange and still smelled of its factory. Bastian looked at it with suspicion.

"The Tern had more character," he said.

"The Tern had a cracked heat shield," said Yusra.

They held a second, longer service for Haruki once the new crew were aboard, and this time the new pilot read the poem.

Afterwards Yusra wrote the rotation report. Commander Yusra Adeyemi, forty-eight, she typed, and then sat looking at the number for a long time. She felt older than that. She felt about a hundred.

In the end she wrote a single line about the storm of Day 209, the regulator and the three of them who had kept the station alive, and recommended Bastian Kowal for promotion. Then she went to find him, to tell him to stop singing.
