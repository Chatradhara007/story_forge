Chapter Three

Day 215. The main oxygen regulator was holding, but it whined in a way Bastian did not like.

He came to the hub in the morning without his beard. He had shaved it off in the night, he explained, because it would not let the helmet seal properly, and if the regulator went he would be outside fixing it in a suit. His face looked naked and very young.

"You look twelve," said Yusra.

"I feel about twelve," said Bastian.

Ana Reyes came up the ladder to the hub at a run, two rungs at a time, with a tablet in her hand. Her leg was splinted from ankle to knee.

"The regulator readings," she said. "Bastian's right. It's failing. Days, not weeks."

Yusra looked at the numbers. Thirteen days until the supply drop. No spare, no shuttle, no pilot.

"All six of us are going to have to cut our oxygen use," she said. "Sleep more. Move less. No more running." She looked at Bastian. "And no more singing. Singing uses air."

That evening they held a small service for Haruki in the hub. Bastian read something from a book of poems. Ana sat with her leg up on a crate. Yusra said a few words about the best pilot she had ever flown with, and then they turned off the lights and sat in the dark for a minute, which was what Haruki had always said he wanted.
