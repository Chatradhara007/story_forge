Chapter Two

The solar storm hit Kestrel on Day 214, nine hours earlier than the forecast said it would.

The alarms went first, then the lights. Yusra was in the hub; Bastian was in the reactor ring; Haruki was outside in the Tern, coming back from a survey of the outer antenna.

"Haruki, get in," Yusra said into the comm. "Now."

The Tern came in too fast. The docking clamps missed, the shuttle struck the edge of the bay and broke apart, and the storm took what was left. Yusra watched it happen on the bay camera. There was nothing anybody could do. Haruki Mori was dead before the alarms stopped.

In the chaos Ana was thrown down the access ladder to the medical deck. She lay at the bottom with her right leg bent under her, swearing steadily, until Bastian found her.

"It's broken," she said. "Splint it, help me to the bed, and don't you dare sing."

A coolant line in the stores section had ruptured in the surge, and the cold was spreading. To save the rest of the section, Bastian had to blow the outer hatch. Everything in the stores bay went out into the dark: food crates, spare suits, and the station's only spare oxygen regulator, tumbling away toward Tarsis.

When it was over, Yusra sat on the floor of the hub with her back to the wall. There were three of them now.
